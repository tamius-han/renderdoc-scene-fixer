Controls
Control scheme

Key bindings:

Camera:
Num 1 / Num 3 / Num 7 — front/side/top view
Num 9 — flip view
Num 5 — toggle orthographic/perspective
A — toggle fly mode
Fly mode:
ESDF — movement (fly mode)
Space — up; Ctrl — down
Selection:
Left Click — select
Shift Left Click — multi-select
Ctrl Left Click — add/remove from selection
Right Click (off-object) — clear selection
ESC — clear selection
Ctrl I — invert selection
Misc.
H — hide selected
Ctrl Shift E — export selection
Ctrl E — export selection
